A Transparent Process,
Phase by Phase

No black boxes. Here is exactly what happens between your first conversation and a running, improving operation — and what you'll see at every stage.

Stage 1 — Plan

Get It Right Before It Starts

  1. Discovery

    A structured consultation about your business: goals, offer, customers, current bottlenecks, and what success looks like in 90 days.

  2. Requirements

    We translate that into a written scope: roles, coverage hours, tools, KPI definitions, communication cadence — and a confirmed timeline and pricing proposal.

Stage 2 — Build

Your Team, Assembled Properly

  1. Recruitment

    We source candidates against your specific role profile — experience, language ability, and attitude screened deliberately.

  2. Screening

    Multi-stage evaluation: skills assessment, English communication checks, scenario exercises, and reference verification. Strong finalists are presented for your approval.

  3. Training

    Role-specific fundamentals plus onboarding on your offer, scripts, objection handling, systems, and standards — before any live work touches your customers.

Stage 3 — Launch

Go Live Without the Chaos

  1. Technology Setup

    CRM access, dialer, email, calendars, and reporting dashboards configured and tested before day one.

  2. Deployment

    Your team goes live against agreed targets with documented processes, escalation paths, and coverage aligned to your U.S. business hours.

Stage 4 — Operate & Grow

Where Most Providers Stop. We Start.

  1. Management

    Daily supervision, scheduling, and coaching by DB Virtual Solutions team leaders — you set strategy, we run the floor.

  2. Quality Assurance

    Sampled call and task reviews against scorecards, with feedback loops and corrective-action plans when standards slip.

  3. Reporting

    Regular reporting on the KPIs agreed in your scope — activity, outcomes, QA results, and what we're doing about them.

  4. Optimization

    Data-driven iteration: script refinements, targeting adjustments, process fixes, and targeted coaching.

  5. Scaling

    When results justify it, we extend your team — new roles, added seats, additional functions — under the same managed structure.

Partnership, Not Handoff

What We Need From You

The process above works best when our clients bring five things. None of them take much time:

  • Clear goals for what the team should achieve
  • Your offer details, messaging, and brand voice
  • Access to the tools your team will use
  • A decision-maker available for approvals and reviews
  • Honest feedback early — it makes every later phase better

Your Phase One Starts With a Conversation

You'll leave the consultation with a recommended team structure, a timeline, and transparent next steps — whether or not you move forward.
