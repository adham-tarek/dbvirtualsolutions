Cookie & Similar Technologies Policy

Effective: August 31, 2026
Last updated: August 31, 2026

This Policy explains how DB Virtual Solutions (“DBVS”) uses cookies and similar website technologies on dbvirtualsolutions.com.

Current Website Use

DBVS does not currently use advertising cookies or behavioral-advertising pixels on the Site.

The current Site configuration does not enable:

  • Google Analytics;
  • Meta Pixel; or
  • LinkedIn advertising/insight tracking.

The Site may still rely on ordinary hosting, security, content-delivery, and technical infrastructure necessary to operate the website.

Those providers may process normal technical requests as part of delivering or protecting the Site.

The Site uses Google Fonts to display its typography. Loading these fonts sends technical requests, including your IP address and browser information, to Google.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small data files that websites or service providers may store or access on a device.

Similar technologies can include local storage, pixels, and other technical mechanisms.

Some technologies are necessary for website functionality or security, while others may be used for analytics or advertising.

DBVS currently does not deploy non-essential advertising or analytics trackers through this Site.

Managing Cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings.

Blocking technologies necessary for website operation may affect functionality.

Because DBVS does not currently use advertising or analytics cookies, the Site does not currently require a DBVS advertising-cookie preference center.

Future Changes

If DBVS introduces analytics, advertising, retargeting, or other non-essential tracking technologies in the future, we will update this Policy and implement any notice, consent, opt-out, or privacy controls required by applicable law before using them.
