FAQ

Straight Answers Before You Talk to Us

How does your outsourcing model work?

We recruit, train, and manage dedicated professionals who work exclusively on your account. Our supervisors handle day-to-day management, quality assurance, and reporting, while you set priorities and strategy through a single point of contact.

Where are your teams based?

Our teams are based in Egypt, with working hours scheduled to align with your U.S. time zone.

Can I hire one person, or do I need a full team?

Either. Many engagements start with a single dedicated team member and grow as results justify expansion.

Do you train your employees?

Yes. Every team member completes role-specific training plus onboarding on your offer, scripts, systems, and standards — followed by ongoing coaching.

Who manages the team day to day?

DB Virtual Solutions does — scheduling, supervision, coaching, and QA. You stay in control of goals and priorities and receive regular performance reporting.

What industries do you serve?

Our model is built around B2B companies, real estate, roofing and home services, solar, and other high-value service businesses — and it adapts to most industries that need sales or customer operations capacity. Compliance-sensitive campaigns are evaluated individually during scoping.

How quickly can a team be deployed?

Timelines depend on role complexity, seniority, and team size. Structured onboarding typically takes a matter of weeks; you receive a confirmed timeline in your proposal before anything begins.

How do you monitor quality?

Through structured QA: sampled call and task reviews, scorecards, coaching sessions, KPI dashboards, and corrective-action plans when performance drifts.

How does pricing work?

Pricing is customized per engagement based on role, hours, and team structure. There is no public rate card — book a consultation and we'll prepare a tailored proposal.
