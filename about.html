Our Story

The Right Talent. The Wrong Assumptions.

Egypt holds one of the largest pools of educated, trainable, English-speaking professionals in the region — university graduates with strong communication skills, work ethic, and genuine appetite for careers in international business.

Too often, that talent gets buried under the worst version of outsourcing: anonymous call-center floors, high-pressure scripts, no development, no standards — and predictable results.

DB Virtual Solutions started from a simple conviction: if you combine this talent with structured management and U.S.-market expectations, you can give American businesses something genuinely rare — a dedicated team that performs like an in-house department, at a cost structure that makes sustained growth possible.

So that's what we build. Every hire is screened and trained properly. Every team is managed and measured. Every client gets transparency about how their operation actually runs.

Who we serve

U.S. small and medium-sized businesses, entrepreneurs, and sales-driven service companies — from a first dedicated hire to full managed operations.

Where we operate

Delivery hub: Egypt. Client focus: United States, with team schedules aligned to U.S. time zones.

How we work with you

Named point of contact, agreed KPIs, regular reporting, and a management layer that treats your standards as ours.
