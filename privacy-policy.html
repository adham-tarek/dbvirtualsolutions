Privacy Policy

Effective: August 31, 2026
Last updated: August 31, 2026

DB Virtual Solutions (“DBVS,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you visit dbvirtualsolutions.com, contact us, or inquire about our services.

Information We Collect

We may collect information that you choose to provide, such as:

  • your name;
  • email address;
  • phone number;
  • company name;
  • information about your business or service needs; and
  • information included in messages or consultation requests.

The Site may also generate ordinary technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages, timestamps, and similar website or security data.

Please do not submit passwords, payment-card information, government identification numbers, health information, or other sensitive personal information through our general inquiry forms.

How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • respond to inquiries;
  • discuss potential services or engagements;
  • communicate with prospective or existing business contacts;
  • operate, maintain, and secure the Site;
  • prevent fraud, misuse, or security incidents;
  • improve our website and business communications; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

Submitting an inquiry does not automatically subscribe you to marketing communications.

Consultation Form

The Site may use an email-based consultation form.

Where no server-side form endpoint is configured, completing the form prepares an email in your email application. DBVS does not receive the information merely because you completed the form. We receive it when you choose to send the email and it reaches our email system.

How We Share Information

We may share information with service providers that support our website, hosting, communications, security, or business operations when reasonably necessary.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect legal rights, or in connection with a legitimate business transaction such as a reorganization or transfer of business assets.

DBVS does not currently sell personal information.

DBVS does not currently share website visitor information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Cookies and Website Technologies

The Site may use technologies necessary for normal website delivery, security, and functionality.

The Site uses Google Fonts to display its typography. Loading these fonts sends technical requests, including your IP address and browser information, to Google.

DBVS does not currently enable Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, LinkedIn advertising tracking, or similar behavioral-advertising technologies on this Site.

For additional information, see our Cookie & Similar Technologies Policy.

Data Retention and Security

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, legitimate business recordkeeping, security, dispute resolution, or legal requirements.

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the information we handle.

However, no website, email system, or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live and which privacy laws apply, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information.

Applicable laws may also provide additional rights regarding the use or disclosure of personal information.

To make a privacy request, contact:

info@dbvirtualsolutions.com

We may request reasonable information to verify your identity before completing a request.

California Privacy Information

California residents may have additional privacy rights when applicable California privacy law applies.

These may include rights to know, correct, or delete personal information and, where applicable, to opt out of certain sales or sharing of personal information.

DBVS does not currently sell personal information or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising through this Site.

Some browsers provide a “Do Not Track” signal. Because there is no universal standard for legacy Do Not Track signals, the Site does not currently change its ordinary behavior solely in response to that signal.

Where applicable law requires recognition of a legally valid privacy preference signal, DBVS will handle that signal as required by applicable law.

Children

The Site is intended for business users and adults. It is not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly use the Site to collect personal information from children.

Third-Party Services

The Site may contain links to third-party websites or rely on third-party infrastructure.

Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, and DBVS is not responsible for third-party privacy practices.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, business practices, or legal requirements change.

The current version will always display its latest revision date.
